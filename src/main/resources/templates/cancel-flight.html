Cancelling removes the flight from the calendar, the itinerary and the flights list, and drops it from the schedule-problems report — so a journey it appeared to make may show up as a travel gap again, and it will no longer be offered as an end for a ground transfer. The event log keeps the record, including the reason. There is no undo: a future flight can be entered again as a new booking, and a past one cannot be entered at all.
