Jfokus is a general topics conference for a developer and tech audience. Jfokus conference, set to take place on February 8-10, 2027, at Stockholm Waterfront Congress Centre.

Jokus is known for its great program and world class speakers.The past year had demonstrated the strength of the community, as evidenced by our sold-out conference with ~2000 attendees.

As we prepare for Jfokus, we are excited to share new trends and technologies that will shape the future of the industry. Our focus remains on Java, the JVM platform, and open-source technologies. However, our program will include cutting-edge topics such as AI, Machine Learning, Big Data, Cloud, Web/HTML5, Agile, NoSQL, DevOps, and emerging languages.

By embracing these trends, we aim to deliver an immersive and diverse experience for our attendees.

Jfokus is three days with six parallell tracks incl Hands-on-Lab, Deep Dive session, 45 minutes technical sessions and 15 minutes lightning talks

Commercial policy
The use of sessions for marketing or selling products, consulting, or other services is prohibited. This policy does not prevent presenters from referencing specific commercial products or engaging in user roundtable discussions about them. However, the core focus of the presentation should remain non-commercial.

The last day for submitting proposals is 30 September!
We will start selecting talks as soon as we get good proposals. You will maximize your chance for a speaking slot in February if you submit early.

Travel expenses
Travel expenses could be covered for some cases, but normally not. We have a limited budget for travel expenses evaluated on a case by case basis. If travel expenses is essential for you to be able to speak at Jfokus, please state budget and if this is a show stopper for you in the comment.

We will get back to you as soon as we have decided which presentations that will be accepted for Jfokus.
