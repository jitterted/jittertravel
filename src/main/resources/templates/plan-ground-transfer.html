Plan Ground Transfer

A short hop with no booking — the taxi from the airport to the hotel, the subway back. It fills a missing-travel gap on Schedule Problems. Anonymous calendar visitors see only “Ground transfer” and the airport code or city at each end — never a hotel or station name, an address, or a time.

Nothing to travel between yet. Both ends are picked from places already recorded, so book a flight, a train or a hotel first — there is no free-text option.
Only flights and trains you arrive on are offered here — picking one fills in when it gets in, and a hotel fills in its check-out
Only flights and trains you leave on are offered here — picking one fills in when it goes, and a hotel fills in its check-in

The times are approximate on purpose — normally “just before” or “just after” a flight. Any date is accepted, including today and days already past.
