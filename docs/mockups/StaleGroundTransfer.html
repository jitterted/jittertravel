Stale Ground Transfers

Mockup for docs/GroundTransferEndpointReferencesPlan.md. A ground transfer is stale when the hotel, flight, train or event it was planned from or to is canceled, or has moved off the transfer's day or (for a flight or train) off its time. Each surface below is drawn with the real page's CSS. The example is Nov 20: the transfer from the palace tour still goes to the Berlin hotel that was canceled and replaced.

Where each part stands
  • M1, Schedule Problems: approved. Leads with the change, icon + kind label, fixes Change / Cancel.
  • M2, cancel pages: approved. Built from a new small projector.
  • M3, itinerary: design A and all its wording approved (Ted, 2026-11-17).
  • M4, Plan Ground Transfer: approved.
  • M5, Change Ground Transfer: approved, with "Currently:" in the banner.

M2. Cancel pages: what this leaves behind

Shown only when a transfer goes to or from the thing being canceled, above the reason field. It doesn't block or change the cancel. It's amber, not red, because the transfer can be fixed afterwards. Drawn here on Cancel Hotel. Cancel Flight, Cancel Flight Itinerary, Cancel Train, Cancel Private Event and Decline Conference get the same line.

  • Every sentence starts with the thing that changed, by the name the itinerary already shows for it, and the strip uses the same words as Schedule Problems.
  • Flights and trains say "Flight" or "Train" before the number, because "LH 1234 was canceled" alone doesn't say what LH 1234 is.
  • "No longer on the schedule" covers a declined conference and one dropped after a rejection, without saying which (the decline wasn't the organizer's doing, so "canceled" would be wrong).
  • If both ends are stale, the card shows two strips, the origin's first.

M4. Plan Ground Transfer: a time the train or flight contradicts

Decided (Ted, 2026-11-17): the form refuses it, so a mistyped time never reaches the calendar. Each panel shows what you do and what the page shows after you press the button. Nothing is saved. The error sits under the time that's wrong, in the form's usual red. It names the kind of leg, not its description, so it stays one short line however long a train's name is (Ted, 2026-11-17).

M5. Change Ground Transfer, opened from the fix link

"Change transfer" on the Schedule Problems card lands here. It's the plan form with this transfer's values filled in, under the usual "why you are here" banner. The end that went stale is left empty, because the canceled hotel is no longer one of the choices, and the line under it says what it was. Saving keeps the same transfer and records the change; the problem card goes away.

  • Picking "Leonardo Hotel Berlin Mitte — Berlin · check in Fri Nov 20, 3:00 PM" leaves the times alone, because they're already valid. The page only fills in a time that would otherwise be wrong, as the plan form does today.
  • When the end moved rather than went away (a new check-out date, a train at a new time), it stays selected and the line reads "Now checks out Sun, Nov 22" or "Now arrives 6:10 PM". You change the date or time, not the place.
  • The form is also reachable without a problem, for any transfer, wherever its cancel bin is today. Without the banner it's simply an edit form.
  • "Save changes" matches Change Hotel and Change Train.
  • The banner's second line starts "Currently:" (Ted, 2026-11-17): the page title already says it's a ground transfer, so the line says what the transfer is now, before the change.
