Linked date fields

Mockup for the hotel, train, flight and conference forms. Change the first date and the second one moves by the same amount, so a one-night stay stays one night. Try it below; it uses the browser's own date inputs, so it behaves as it will on the iPad.

When the second date moves

  • When you finish the year: typing 2027 moves it as soon as the fourth digit lands. The half-typed years in between (0002, 0020, 0202) are ignored.
  • When you leave the field: tab out, tap elsewhere, or close the iPad picker. This covers a changed day or month, which on its own does not move anything while you are still typing.
  • By the same amount: check-in moves 98 days later, so check-out moves 98 days later. Its time of day stays the same.
  • Until you edit the second date yourself: after that it stays where you put it, so fixing a typo in check-in cannot undo the checkout you chose.
