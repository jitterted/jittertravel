Itinerary Check-Out Order

Mockup for /itinerary. A check-out time means the latest you can leave. When a train, flight or ground transfer leaves that city earlier the same day, the Check-Out card moves up to sit just above it. Everything else keeps its clock order.

Rule: a Check-Out sorts at its own time, or at the departure of the first leg leaving that city that day, whichever is earlier. On a tie it goes first.
Label: the time line always reads by 11:00 AM, so a card above a 9:45 AM train doesn't look out of order.
Events don't move it. A breakfast or a meetup in the same city might come before you check out, or you might go back to the room after it. The page can't tell, so it uses clock order.
Dashed outline: the card that changed position (mockup marker only, not on the real page) Edit and cancel icons are left out of these cards.
